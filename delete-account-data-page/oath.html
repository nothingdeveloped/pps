Delete your Oath account and data

Oath is an accountability app made by Silicon. You can delete everything Oath holds about you, either in the app or by asking us by email.

What Oath holds

On your phoneYour name, vows, streak, history and proof photos. Proof photos stay on the phone and are never uploaded.
In your Google DriveOnly if you turn on sync: one hidden file in Drive's app data folder. Only Oath can read it. It is your file, in your Drive, not on our servers.
Your Google sign-inOnly if you sign in with Google: your email address and Google account id. They link your Plus purchase to you so it follows you to a new phone.
PurchasesPayments are handled by Google Play. Our subscription service, Adapty, records whether you have Plus.
AnalyticsApp usage events and crash reports tied to an anonymous install id, not to your name or email.

Option 1: delete it yourself in the app

  1. Erase your vows and history. Open Settings, tap Delete data, then confirm. This erases your vows, streak and history from the phone. If Drive sync is on, it also overwrites the synced file.
  2. Remove the Drive file. Open Settings → Plan & sync → Manage sync and tap Delete cloud data. Then tap Revoke Drive access to remove Oath's permission from your Google account.
  3. Sign out. Open Settings → Plan & sync and tap Sign out under Google account.
  4. Uninstall Oath to remove everything left on the phone.

You can also remove Oath's access from your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Option 2: ask us to delete your data

Email developers.silicon@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Oath data. Send it from the Google account email you signed in with, so we can find your record.

We will delete the records linked to you, including your Google email and account id and your subscription profile, and confirm by email. We aim to do this within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your Google email and account id, the link to your Plus profile, and anything else we hold that can be tied to you.
  • Your own copies: data on your phone and in your Google Drive is under your control. We cannot reach it. Use Option 1 to remove it.
  • Kept: payment and tax records that Google Play or the law require to be kept. Google Play holds these, not Oath.
  • Anonymous analytics cannot be linked back to a person. Uninstalling the app stops all collection.
