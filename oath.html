Privacy Policy

Oath is an accountability app made by Silicon ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app and our related services handle, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 7 October 2026

The short version. Your vows, streak and proof photos stay on your phone. Proof photos are never uploaded. We do not sell your personal information. A few optional features, such as Google sign-in, Drive backup and Premium, involve Google and our subscription provider, and this policy describes exactly how.

1. Information we handle

Your nameThe name you type on your oath page. It is kept on your phone and shown only to you.
Vows, streak and historyThe vows you swear, your schedule, your streak and your record of kept and missed days. Stored on your phone.
Proof photosPhotos you take in the app to prove a vow. They are stored on your phone only. They are never uploaded to our servers or to any third party.
App usage on your phoneIf you create a vow that limits screen time, Oath reads how long apps were used, using Android's Usage access permission. The check runs on your phone and the usage data is not uploaded.
Google accountOptional. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and Google account id. They link your Premium purchase to you so it follows you to a new phone.
Drive backupOptional. If you turn on sync, Oath saves one hidden file in your own Google Drive's app data folder. Only Oath can read it. It is stored in your Drive, not on our servers.
PurchasesPayments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your card details. Our subscription service, Adapty, records whether you have Premium and which plan.
Analytics and crash reportsUsage events (for example, screens opened and features used), device and app details such as model, operating system and app version, and crash logs. These are tied to an anonymous install id, not to your name or email.
Messages to usIf you email us, we keep your message and address to reply.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, messages, call history or health data, and Oath does not use advertising identifiers to show you ads.

2. Phone permissions

  • Camera: to take proof photos. You can turn it off in your phone's settings, but photo vows then cannot be completed.
  • Usage access: only for vows that limit screen time. Optional.
  • Notifications: to remind you when a vow window opens and closes.
  • Exact alarms and start on boot: so reminders still arrive on time after you restart your phone.

You can change any permission at any time in your phone's settings.

3. How we use information

  • To run the app: track your vows, check your proof, keep your streak and send reminders.
  • To provide Premium: confirm your purchase and restore it on a new phone.
  • To back up and sync your data to your own Drive, if you turn that on.
  • To understand how Oath is used, fix crashes and improve the app.
  • To respond to your messages and requests.
  • To protect the app against fraud and misuse, and to meet legal obligations.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising. We use these service providers, and each handles only what is needed for its role:

GoogleGoogle Play (distribution and payments), Google Sign-In, Google Drive (your optional backup), and Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (usage and crash reports).
AdaptySubscription management: your Premium status and purchase records.
PostHogProduct analytics and experiments, using an anonymous install id.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or as part of a sale or merger of our business. In that case we would tell you and this policy would continue to apply to your information.

5. How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you delete it in the app or uninstall Oath.
  • In your Google Drive: until you delete the backup in the app or from your Drive.
  • Account and subscription records: while your account is active, and for no more than three months after you ask us to delete it, unless the law requires us to keep payment or tax records longer.
  • Analytics and crash reports: only as long as needed for those purposes, under our providers' retention settings.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. Keeping proof photos on your phone and your backup in your own Drive means most of your data never sits on our systems. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

Oath is intended for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and parts of the United States, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where we rely on it;
  • not be treated differently for using these rights.

To use any of these rights, email us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days. You can delete your data yourself in the app, and the steps are on our Delete your Oath account and data page.

You can also turn off optional features at any time: sign out of Google, disconnect Drive, revoke Usage access, or turn off notifications.

If you are in the UK or EEA and are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to your local data protection authority.

9. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. If we make a significant change, we will tell you in the app or by another suitable method.
